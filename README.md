# Brave vs Chrome: Which Browser Is Better in 2026?

Chrome is still the world's most used browser, but Brave keeps growing. Brave reached a record **130.77 million monthly active users** in October 2026, with daily users peaking at **55.42 million**. Its search engine, Brave Search, handled **2.71 billion queries** that month, according to [PiunikaWeb](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/11/03/brave-130-million-monthly-active-users/).

Chrome is still far bigger. It accounted for **68.6% of human web traffic** in Q3 2026, including **69.2% on mobile** and **67.9% on desktop**, according to [Technology Checker's analysis of Cloudflare Radar data](https://technologychecker.io/blog/chrome-market-share). Because Brave is built on Chromium and identifies itself as Chrome, its traffic is usually counted inside Chrome's share.

So which should you use? In this Brave vs Chrome guide, I'll compare both browsers on speed, privacy, ad blocking, extensions, AI and battery life, and help you decide which one is better for you in 2026.

## What Is the Difference Between Brave and Chrome?

**Chrome is Google's browser, focused on speed and deep integration with Google services, while Brave is a privacy-first browser that blocks ads and trackers by default.**

- **Chrome:** Made by Google, huge extension library, tight Google account sync
- **Brave:** Built on Chromium, blocks ads and trackers by default, offers Brave Rewards, Brave Search and Leo AI

Both use the same Chromium engine, so most websites and Chrome extensions work in both.

## How Many People Use Brave vs Chrome?

**Chrome has billions of users and around two-thirds of web traffic, while Brave has about 131 million monthly users.**

- **Brave:** 130.77 million monthly active users and 55.42 million peak daily users in October 2026
- **Chrome:** About 68.6% of global human web traffic in Q3 2026

Brave has grown fast, from about **80 million monthly users in January 2025** to **109 million in February 2026**, according to [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/brave-blocks-your-ads-and-sells-its-own-and-its-working/). It reached **126 million** by August 2026, before passing 130 million in October, according to [PiunikaWeb's August report](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/09/03/brave-browser-126-million-users-august-2026/). For a full breakdown of how many people use Brave browser, plus Brave revenue and growth over time, see these [Brave browser statistics](https://bloggervoice.com/brave-browser-statistics/).

## Chrome vs Brave: Privacy

**Brave is much better for privacy because it blocks ads, trackers and fingerprinting by default, while Chrome relies more on settings and extensions.**

**Brave privacy features:**
- Brave Shields blocks ads and trackers automatically
- Fingerprinting protection
- Private windows with Tor
- No Google account required

**Chrome privacy features:**
- Safe Browsing protection
- Incognito mode
- Privacy settings and third-party cookie controls
- Strong security updates

If privacy matters to you, Brave is the clear winner in the Brave vs Google Chrome comparison.

## Is Brave Faster Than Chrome?

**Brave often feels faster than Chrome on ad-heavy websites because it blocks ads and trackers before they load, but on clean sites both browsers perform similarly.**

- **Ad-heavy sites:** Brave usually loads pages faster because there's less to download
- **Simple sites:** Speed is very similar, since both use Chromium
- **Data use:** Brave often uses less data by blocking ads

So, is Brave faster than Chrome? In everyday browsing with lots of ads, usually yes.

## Chrome vs Brave: Ad Blocking

**Brave blocks ads by default, while Chrome needs an ad blocker extension.**

Brave also blocks many YouTube ads in the browser. Chrome's switch to newer extension rules (Manifest V3) has limited some ad blockers, which has pushed some users toward Brave.

## Brave Browser vs Chrome: Extensions

**Both support Chrome Web Store extensions, so you can use most of the same add-ons.**

Chrome has the edge in deep Google integration, while Brave runs most Chrome extensions with fewer built-in trackers.

## Brave vs Chrome: AI Features

**Chrome uses Google's Gemini AI features, while Brave offers Leo, its privacy-focused AI assistant.**

- **Chrome:** Gemini-powered tools for summaries, writing help and search
- **Brave:** Leo AI for summaries, questions and writing, with a focus on privacy

## Brave vs Google Chrome: Battery and Memory

**Brave can use less battery and memory on ad-heavy websites because it blocks extra scripts, though results vary by device.**

## Brave vs Chrome: Sync and Google Services

**Chrome is better if you rely heavily on Google services like Gmail, Drive and Google Pay, while Brave offers its own private sync.**

## Brave vs Chrome: Rewards and Business Model

**Chrome is supported by Google's ad business, while Brave earns money from privacy-respecting ads, search, premium products and crypto partnerships.**

Brave users can opt in to Brave Rewards and see private ads in exchange for BAT tokens. Brave CEO Brendan Eich says the company gets by on its search API, ads, premium products and crypto deals. For more on Brave revenue, valuation and Brave browser market share, see the full [Brave browser market share](https://bloggervoice.com/brave-browser-statistics/) breakdown.

## Brave vs Chrome: Pros and Cons

**Brave pros:**
- Blocks ads and trackers by default
- Often faster on ad-heavy sites
- Strong privacy features
- Supports Chrome extensions
- Built-in Brave Search and Leo AI

**Brave cons:**
- Some sites may break with Shields on
- Crypto and rewards features aren't for everyone
- Smaller company and community than Google

**Chrome pros:**
- Most popular and widely supported browser
- Deep Google integration
- Strong security updates
- Huge extension library

**Chrome cons:**
- Collects more data by default
- Needs extensions for ad blocking
- Can use more memory on heavy sites

## Which Browser Should You Choose?

**Choose Brave for privacy, ad blocking and speed on ad-heavy sites. Choose Chrome if you depend on Google services and want maximum compatibility.**

- **Best for privacy:** Brave
- **Best for blocking ads:** Brave
- **Best for Google users:** Chrome
- **Best for work tools and compatibility:** Chrome
- **Best for saving mobile data:** Brave

## How to Switch From Chrome to Brave

**Install Brave, import your Chrome bookmarks, passwords and extensions, and set Brave as your default browser.**

1. **Download Brave** from brave.com
2. **Import data** from Chrome during setup
3. **Reinstall extensions** from the Chrome Web Store
4. **Turn on Brave Sync** for other devices
5. **Set Brave as default** in your device settings

## Final Thoughts

Brave vs Chrome comes down to privacy versus convenience. Brave blocks ads and trackers by default, often loads ad-heavy pages faster and now has more than 130 million monthly users. Chrome remains the most used browser in the world, with about two-thirds of web traffic and the deepest Google integration.

If you care about privacy and fewer ads, Brave is the better choice in 2026. If you rely heavily on Google services, Chrome may still suit you better. Since both use the same engine, you can easily try Brave without losing your favorite extensions.

## FAQs

### Is Brave better than Chrome?
Brave is better for privacy and ad blocking, while Chrome is better for Google integration and compatibility.

### Is Brave faster than Chrome?
On ad-heavy websites, Brave is often faster because it blocks ads and trackers. On simple sites, both are similar.

### How many people use Brave browser?
Brave had about 130.77 million monthly active users in October 2026.

### Is Brave safer than Chrome?
Brave offers stronger privacy by default, while both browsers receive regular security updates.

### Can I use Chrome extensions in Brave?
Yes. Brave supports most extensions from the Chrome Web Store.

### Does Brave block YouTube ads?
Brave blocks many YouTube ads by default, though results can vary as YouTube changes its systems.

### What is Brave's market share?
Brave is usually counted inside Chrome's share because it identifies as Chrome, so its exact market share is hard to measure.

### Is Brave owned by Google?
No. Brave is an independent company co-founded by Brendan Eich. It's built on Chromium, the open-source project behind Chrome.
